Before You Order

  • Please double-check your chosen PJ style and size before placing your order.
  • The customer is responsible for selecting the correct size.
  • Because personalised items are made specifically to your requirements, an incorrect size selected by the customer cannot normally be resold or exchanged.
  • Once your personalised order has been confirmed, changes may not be possible.
  • If you are unsure about sizing, please contact Me To You Designs before ordering and we will be happy to help.
Please note: This policy does not affect your statutory rights where an item is faulty, damaged, or does not match the order you placed.
